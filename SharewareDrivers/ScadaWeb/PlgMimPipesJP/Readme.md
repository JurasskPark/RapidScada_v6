Adds pipe layouts and equipment indicators to Rapid SCADA mimic diagrams.

## Highlights

- straight and narrow straight pipes;
- 45° and 90° elbows;
- tee, cross and Y branches;
- U-bend and S-offset;
- concentric reducer, expansion joint and flexible section;
