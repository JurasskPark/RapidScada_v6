Create SVG equipment symbols for Rapid SCADA mimic diagrams and animate their details using channel values. Draw a pump, valve or process unit in the built-in designer, assign channels and configure operator actions.

## Highlights

- Editable shapes, paths, groups, local gradients and SVG import.
- Eleven animated properties with conditions, ranges, cycles and numeric text.
- Channel roles, a channel catalog and reusable faceplates.
- Commands, charts and navigation on individual symbol details.
- A built-in simulator and 36 HelloWorld examples.
