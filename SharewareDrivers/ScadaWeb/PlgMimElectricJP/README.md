Adds electrical, instrumentation, automation and low-current symbols to Rapid SCADA mimic diagrams. Build equipment diagrams in the editor and display their states and measurements from SCADA channels.

## Highlights

- 101 component types in nine groups: electrical supply, control, instrumentation, PLC/I/O, cabling, fire, security, communications and custom devices.
- Channel-driven positions, trips, alarms and analog values.
- A fixed 50 px grid, rotation and engineering connection metadata.
- Industrial, IEC-oriented and project-specific drawing profiles.
- An autonomous demonstration of a motor starter and a source selector.
