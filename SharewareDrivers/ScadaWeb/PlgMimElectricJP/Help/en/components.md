The separate [ElectricalDemo](demo.md) is not included in the 101-type count.

Dimensions on group pages are fixed logical pixels before rotation. State profiles are identifiers explained in the [state reference](states.md). A static type uses `None`. For channels and engineering properties see [bindings](channels.md) and [parameters](parameters.md).

To show optional groups, configure [package switches](configuration.md). Hiding a group does not unload its saved components.
